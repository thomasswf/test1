What I work on

Reliability is a system, not a single metric.

Availability & incident learning

I focus on what happens after an incident as much as what happened during it.

  • Root-cause analysis and evidence quality
  • Containment and corrective-action governance
  • Recurrence prevention and trend learning
  • Risk review and closure discipline

Electrical & mechanical infrastructure

A strong program depends on understanding the equipment and failure modes beneath the dashboard.

  • Power distribution, UPS and protection
  • Cooling and mechanical resilience
  • Commissioning and integrated testing
  • Operational maintainability and fault response

Program leadership

Regional reliability work rarely belongs to one team. I enjoy creating structure across functions without losing technical depth.

  • Cross-functional ownership
  • Technical review and challenge
  • Metrics, follow-up and escalation
  • Turning complex issues into clear actions

Engineering judgment

Standards matter, but so do context, failure consequence and how systems are actually operated.

  • Risk-based decision making
  • Protection philosophy and failure paths
  • Design versus operational reality
  • Learning from weak signals before they become events

How I think

Engineering principles I keep returning to.

“A good RCA does more than explain the past. It changes the probability of the next failure.”

Evidence before narrative

Build the sequence from facts, logs, measurements and physical behavior before deciding what story fits.

Interfaces are where risk hides

Many failures emerge between teams, systems, procedures and assumptions rather than inside a single component.

Corrective actions must change reality

An action is useful only if it removes, detects, contains or meaningfully reduces the failure mechanism.

Keep technical depth connected to outcomes

Senior engineering work means being able to move between a breaker setting and a regional risk decision without losing either context.
